 Edlink currently handles examinations through this workflow:

  1. Exam creation

  The administrator or academic officer goes to Exams → Create Exam and provides:

  - Exam name, such as Mid-Term or End-of-Term
  - Target class
  - Optional stream
  - Current academic term

  The term must be open. The exam starts as a draft.

  2. Subject papers

  Subject papers are added to the exam individually. Each paper has:

  - Subject
  - Maximum score
  - Weighting

  For example, Mathematics may have a maximum of 100 and a weighting of 1. A paper added again is updated rather than
  duplicated.

  3. Learners included

  Edlink includes active learners from the exam’s class and optional stream.

  For S3–S6, the individual subject-selection module is also applied:

  - A learner only appears on the marks sheet for subjects they selected.
  - Unselected subjects do not create missing-mark errors.
  - S1–S2 and schools without configured selections continue using class-wide subjects.

  4. Marks entry

  An assigned subject teacher or authorized academic administrator goes to Exams → Enter Marks.

  They can:

  - Select their assigned paper
  - Enter scores for eligible learners
  - Save incomplete marks as a draft
  - Submit completed marks for approval

  A submitted paper requires a score for every eligible learner. Scores cannot be negative or exceed the paper’s maximum
  score.

  5. Marks approval

  Submitted marks are reviewed by someone with exam-management permissions.

  They can:

  - Approve submitted marks
  - Reopen approved marks for corrections

  Only approved papers are included in official results and report cards.

  6. Results calculation

  For each subject, Edlink calculates:

  Percentage = learner score ÷ maximum score × 100

  The overall weighted average is calculated using each paper’s weighting:

  Weighted average = Σ(score × weighting) ÷ Σ(maximum score × weighting) × 100

  The system then applies the grading scale configured for the learner’s education stage:

  - Primary
  - O-Level
  - A-Level
  - Kindergarten

  Class positions are generated from weighted averages, with learners having the same average receiving the same
  position.

  7. Publication checks

  Before results can be published, Edlink verifies that:

  - The examination has subject papers
  - Every paper has been approved
  - Grading scales cover the complete percentage range
  - Active learners exist
  - Every eligible learner has all required marks

  A missing mark prevents publication. An unselected subject for an S3–S6 learner does not count as missing.

  8. Publishing results

  Once ready, an administrator can publish the examination.

  Published results become available to linked students and parents through My Results. An administrator can also
  unpublish the results while the term remains open.

  9. Report cards

  Report cards can include:

  - Subject scores and percentages
  - Grades and remarks
  - Weighted average
  - Aggregate points
  - Class position
  - Attendance
  - Fees paid, amount due and balance
  - Promotion outcome
  - Teacher remarks

  The exact contents depend on the school’s report settings.

  10. Fee-clearance control

  A school can require fee clearance before parents or students access published results. Staff can still review
  internal results.

  One current weakness is that the exam setup subject dropdown shows every subject registered by the school, rather than
  only subjects assigned to the selected class. That should be tightened to prevent an administrator from accidentally
  adding an unrelated subject paper.

  - Changes the school from demo to active.
  - Removes the expired-demo restriction.
  - Clears the old demo expiration date.
  - Applies the selected package and learner limit.
  - Preserves the school’s users, students, settings, and existing data.
  - Records the change in the platform audit log.
